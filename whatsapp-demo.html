    One-time setup for Jayshree

    એક વાર ગોઠવણી

    All of this is in the free WhatsApp Business app, under Settings, then Business tools. About one afternoon, with help.

    • Install WhatsApp BusinessA separate free app. She can keep her current number or use a new one for the shop.
    • Fill in the business profileShop name, city, hours, a short line about her sarees.
    • Add each saree to the catalogPhoto, name, price and code (like GJ-201). Once per saree. Customers can then add them to a cart.
    • Turn on the greeting messageAn automatic hello for new customers that points them to the catalog.
    • Save quick repliesShort cuts like /pay for e-Transfer details and /ship for delivery times. One tap instead of typing.
    • Make labelsNew order, Paid, Shipped. She marks each chat so nothing gets lost.
    • Make a broadcast listRegular customers who saved her number. She sends new arrivals to all of them at once.
    • Link it from the websiteThe website's green buttons open this chat. We add her number once and it all connects.

    Good to know before starting

    Payment stays outside WhatsApp

    WhatsApp can't take money in Canada. The customer pays by Interac e-Transfer and Jayshree checks her bank before shipping.

    Orders come in as chat messages

    There is no order screen. Labels (New order, Paid, Shipped) are how she keeps track.

    Broadcasts only reach people who saved her number

    Each broadcast list holds up to 256 people. That is why the website asks customers to save the number and say hi.

    Photos sell the saree

    Daylight, the full saree plus a close-up of the border and pallu. The same photos go on the website and in the catalog.
