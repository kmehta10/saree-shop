Good to know

Questions

Will the colour look the same as the photo?

Phone screens change colours a little. Ask for a video call in daylight and you will see the real colour before paying.

How do I pay?

By Interac e-Transfer once you have confirmed the saree on WhatsApp. Cash is fine for pick-up and home visits.

How long does shipping take?

Canada Post, usually 2 to 6 business days, with a tracking number. Free pick-up in Ajax.

Can I return or exchange?

Exchanges within 7 days if the saree is unused and the tags are on. Stitched blouses can't be exchanged.

Do you ship outside Canada?

Yes, to the USA. Message us for the shipping cost.
