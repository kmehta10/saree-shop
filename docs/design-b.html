Questions people ask me

Will the colour look the same as the photo?

Phone screens change colours a little. Ask me for a video call in daylight and you will see the real colour before paying.

How do I pay?

By Interac e-Transfer after you confirm on WhatsApp. Cash is fine if you pick up from my home.

How long does shipping take?

Canada Post, usually 2 to 6 business days, with a tracking number.

Can I exchange a saree?

Yes, within 7 days if it is unused with the tags on. Stitched blouses can't be exchanged.

I don't know which saree to choose. Can you help?

Of course. Tell me the occasion, your budget and colours you like. I will send you a few choices.
